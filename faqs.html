Frequently Asked Questions

Where are Armina Stone showrooms and slab yards located? Company & Showrooms

We operate three full-service showrooms and indoor slab galleries: our Armina Luxe showroom in Miami, Florida (3635 NE 1st Avenue, Suite 115E), along with our Cheswick (1136 Freeport Rd) and Robinson Township (1000 Park Manor Blvd) locations in Greater Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom or slab warehouse? Company & Showrooms

Walk-ins are always welcome during showroom hours. However, we recommend scheduling a private consultation if you would like a dedicated stone specialist to pull specific slab bundles, review architectural drawings, or walk you through custom finishes and edge profiles.

What is the typical timeline from slab selection to final installation? Fabrication & Install

Once your slabs are selected and on-site digital laser templating is completed, standard fabrication and installation typically take 7 to 14 business days. Custom waterfall islands, full-height bookmatched walls, or large commercial builds may require additional lead time.

Does Armina Stone handle both fabrication and installation? Fabrication & Install

Yes. We manage the entire process in-house—from direct quarry sourcing and slab inspection to precision CNC cutting, hand-finishing, digital laser templating, and white-glove installation by our experienced stone craftsmen.

Can homeowners purchase directly, or do you only work with trade professionals? Company & Showrooms

We partner with both homeowners and trade professionals—including architects, interior designers, custom home builders, and commercial developers. Every client receives the same concierge-level slab selection and project support.

How do I request a quote or reserve a specific stone slab? Company & Showrooms

You can request a personalized quote online, visit any of our showrooms, or share your project dimensions and drawings with our team. Selected slabs can be placed on a complimentary short-term hold while your project details and templating are finalized.

How durable is natural granite for high-traffic kitchen countertops? Products & Materials

Natural granite ranks between 6 and 7 on the Mohs hardness scale, making it one of the most resilient natural surfaces available. It is highly resistant to everyday scratching, chipping, and heat from hot cookware when properly installed and maintained.

Does natural granite need to be sealed, and how often? Care & Maintenance

Most natural granites benefit from a penetrating stone sealer to help resist oil and liquid absorption. Depending on the stone's density and finish, we typically recommend resealing once every 1 to 2 years—a straightforward process that takes just minutes.

What is the difference between Polished, Honed, and Leathered finishes? Products & Materials

A Polished finish offers a mirror-like gloss that amplifies color depth and mineral contrast. Honed provides a smooth, velvety matte appearance with minimal glare. Leathered introduces a subtle, tactile texture that preserves organic character while masking fingerprints and water spots.

Should I choose 2cm or 3cm slab thickness for my project? Products & Materials

3cm slabs are the standard choice for residential kitchen countertops and islands due to their structural strength and bold profile without requiring a laminated edge. 2cm slabs are ideal for vertical applications such as fireplace surrounds, shower walls, full-height backsplashes, or custom mitered edges.

Is bookmatching available for slabs with dramatic veining? Fabrication & Install

Yes. For exotic granites and stones with bold directional movement, we stock consecutive sequential slabs from the same quarry block so our CNC technicians can mirror-match veining seamlessly across waterfall islands and feature walls.

Can natural granite slabs be used outdoors for summer kitchens and bars? Products & Materials

Yes. Granite is UV-stable, freeze-thaw resistant, and naturally impervious to sunlight fading, making it an exceptional choice for outdoor kitchens, BBQ islands, patio bars, and architectural cladding—especially in honed or leathered finishes.

What cleaning products are safe for daily natural stone care? Care & Maintenance

For everyday cleaning, use warm water with a few drops of mild dish soap or a pH-neutral stone cleaner and a soft microfiber cloth. Avoid acidic cleaners (such as vinegar, lemon juice, or bleach) and abrasive scouring pads, which can dull the finish or break down protective sealers over time.

Which countertop edge profiles do you offer? Fabrication & Install

We craft a full suite of architectural edge profiles including Eased (straight modern), Half-Bullnose, Full-Bullnose, Bevel, Demi-Ogee, and thick mitered apron edges for waterfall islands. Your specialist can walk you through physical edge samples during your slab selection.
